CH. 05 — THE VENTURE

Workilee

The idea I keep coming back to: a platform that matches customers with verified skilled workers automatically — not through a phone tree.

CONCEPT / IN DEVELOPMENT
THE PROBLEM

Finding a skilled worker still runs on luck

Ask around, call a few numbers, hope someone's actually available and actually good. There's no verification layer, no live tracking, no rating history to check before someone shows up at your door.

THE IDEA

Match automatically, on real signals

Workilee's matching engine is built to assign the best available worker based on skill, location, availability, and rating — the same signals a good dispatcher would use, minus the phone tree.

The Vision

A platform where "I need a skilled worker" turns into "matched, verified, tracked, paid" — without a single phone call.
